# OODARCS S-Phase Tournament v2

## Decision

Generate and adversarially compare genuinely distinct meanings for an optional **S** operation in OODARC(+S). The outcome may be:

- a semantic phase;
- an enforceable runtime phase;
- an optional side-loop;
- a capability/tool invoked by an existing phase;
- an artifact type;
- consolidation of existing OODARC phases;
- or no S at all.

Do not assume an S deserves a letter. Do not assume current Skaffen implementation defines the ideal semantics.

## Baseline

OODARC currently means Observe, Orient, Decide, Act, Reflect, Compound. Treat it as coupled loops at multiple pace layers, not a mandatory linear sequence. Agent orchestration is an orthogonal topology.

The current leading proposal is **Scout**: search related, lateral, and orthogonal domains for transferable mechanisms, emitting a speculative report with source mechanism, mapping, breakpoints, falsifiable prediction, reversible probe, authority grade, and expiry. Scout output must re-enter ordinary observation/action/validation before promotion.

## Lessons already established; do not spend the tournament rediscovering them

1. A **semantic phase** is a distinct epistemic transformation with a unique input/output/re-entry contract.
2. A **runtime phase** additionally needs enforceable state, capability, routing, or authority differences. Tool-gate delta is a feasibility test, not the sole definition of meaning.
3. Every candidate must be classified separately on semantic distinctness and runtime enforcement. It may pass one and fail the other.
4. Epistemic safety is a hard gate, not a weighted preference. Disqualify any generative candidate that cannot name:
   - the grade carried by speculative output;
   - the store or namespace separating it from validated knowledge;
   - the read filter preventing ordinary Orient/Reflect/Compound from treating it as evidence;
   - its expiry, retirement, and negative-result retention behavior.
5. Do not erase failed probes. Negative transfer results are durable evidence about source-domain base rates even when they are never promoted as true claims.
6. Scout/Search/Speculate/Synthesize are not distinct candidates merely because their verbs differ. Cluster synonyms unless their contracts, triggers, outputs, or authority differ.
7. The no-S null must be specified affirmatively: identify which existing operations/capabilities carry exploration, falsification, simulation, transfer, and retirement, and what would be lost without a new operation.
8. Apply the same distinctness criteria to incumbent OODARC legs. If the criteria imply consolidation, report it, but do not let incumbent implementation archaeology replace the candidate tournament.

## Mandatory balanced seed portfolio

The seed round must cover these five distinct positions. Each seed lens must generate or refine candidate contracts before criticizing the review machinery.

1. **Exploration advocate — ecology/evolution/bandits/optimal foraging.** Make the strongest affirmative case for a sparse exploration operation and generate at least three distinct S contracts. Failure mode to resist: safety-through-never-generating.
2. **Falsification advocate — scientific discovery, severe testing, adversarial inquiry.** Generate candidates centered on disconfirmation, anomaly, and counterexample. Compare generativity with error correction.
3. **Reconnaissance advocate — intelligence analysis/scouting.** Steelman Scout, distinguish collection from interpretation, and specify provenance/expiry.
4. **Learning-transfer advocate — organizational learning, analogical transfer, pedagogy.** Generate candidates for transfer, teaching, propagation, and cross-context adaptation; separate topology from epistemic operation.
5. **Parsimony adversary — cybernetics/control/linguistics.** Steelman no-S, phase consolidation, edge modifiers, and mnemonic quality. It may eliminate candidates only after the other four have produced operational contracts.

A seed set that omits the exploration advocate or contains only anti-S failure modes is invalid and must be corrected before retargeting.

## Candidate-generation requirement

Produce at least twelve raw candidates from the five lenses, then cluster contract-equivalent names. The serious field must include, but is not limited to:

- Scout;
- Speculate;
- Stress-test;
- Simulate;
- Synthesize;
- no S / capability in Orient;
- no S / capability in Compound;
- at least three candidates not named in either charter;
- at least one candidate that primarily subtracts, retires, or prunes rather than generates;
- at least one candidate that changes the exploration/exploitation policy rather than emitting a report.

Do not score until the raw candidates have been clustered into genuinely different operations.

## Per-candidate contract

For every shortlisted contract state:

1. Input and preconditions.
2. Transformation performed.
3. Output artifact and consumer.
4. Authority grade, storage boundary, read filter, expiry, and retention of failed probes.
5. Trigger and natural pace layer.
6. Re-entry into OODARC.
7. Capability/state/routing delta needed for runtime enforcement.
8. Overlap with Observe, Orient, Reflect, and Compound.
9. Characteristic failure mode and Goodhart/gaming risk.
10. Cheapest falsifiable pilot and losing condition.
11. Classification: semantic phase, runtime phase, side-loop, capability, artifact, or no-S null.

## Tournament gates and criteria

### Hard gates

Eliminate a candidate if it lacks:

- semantic distinction from existing operations;
- a falsifiable output or losing condition;
- epistemic separation of speculative and validated material;
- a bounded trigger/stop rule;
- an explicit re-entry path.

A candidate may remain as a tool or artifact after failing phasehood.

### Comparative criteria

For survivors, compare with qualitative scores plus uncertainty:

- generativity beyond local optimization;
- falsification and calibration;
- pace/shear coherence;
- operational crispness;
- implementation and cognitive cost;
- mnemonic/taste;
- resistance to authority laundering;
- value relative to the no-S null.

Do not use false numerical precision. Explain the evidence that would reverse each ranking.

## Mandatory disagreement and fusion work

Before final ranking, explicitly adjudicate:

- Scout vs Speculate: outward search behavior versus epistemic status.
- Generative candidates vs Stress-test: exploration versus disconfirmation.
- Any S vs no-S: new operation versus capability inflation.
- Phase vs side-loop: lifecycle membership versus sparse escalation.
- Synthesize vs Orient/Compound: direct duplication test.
- Additive candidates vs a subtractive/retirement candidate.

Attempt at least two fused lenses:

1. exploration/optimal-foraging × epistemic provenance;
2. cybernetic control × organizational transfer or intelligence reconnaissance.

A fusion counts only if it yields a new contract, discriminator, or conditional recommendation that neither parent supplied.

## Pilot contract independent of current Skaffen quality aggregation

Do not use current `QualitySignal`, token efficiency, phase count, or task-type-partitioned history as the primary outcome.

Design a small pre-registered, read-only evaluation corpus containing tasks with hidden cross-domain mechanisms, tempting false analogies, unresolved contradictions, and cases where no exploration is beneficial. Compare equal-budget traces under:

- baseline OODARC/no-S;
- top candidate A;
- top candidate B.

Blind-score:

- validated mechanism discoveries;
- disconfirmed hypotheses retained;
- false-transfer cost;
- time/turns to useful probe;
- authority-laundering violations;
- unnecessary exploration on no-benefit cases.

Both arms must use identical task buckets and recording paths. Predeclare thresholds and losing conditions. A candidate loses if added exploration fails to improve validated discovery after false-transfer and attention costs.

## Required output

1. Raw longlist and contract-equivalence clusters.
2. Shortlist with full contracts.
3. Hard-gate eliminations.
4. Qualitative comparison matrix with uncertainty.
5. Strongest steelman and attack for each survivor.
6. Disagreement outcomes and genuinely fusion-emergent findings.
7. Conditional recommendation distinguishing semantic phase, runtime phase, and side-loop.
8. Residual human judgments.
9. Minimal pilots for the top two and no-S null.
10. Confidence and explicit reversal conditions.

The goal is the smallest coherent design that improves exploration and learning without creating a competing truth channel or an ornamental seventh step.
