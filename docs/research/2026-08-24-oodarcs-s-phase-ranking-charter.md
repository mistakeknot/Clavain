# OODARC(+S) Final Ranking Charter

## Scope

Finish the comparative half of the completed OODARC(+S) candidate-generation tournament. Read the source synthesis and ledger named in frontmatter. Do **not** restart broad candidate generation or re-audit the charter machinery.

The prior run generated and clustered this live field:

- policy: Fallow, Survey, Slacken, Stint, Season, Divert, Widen;
- subtractive: Reprove, Melt, Retire;
- capability: Assay, Warrant, Trace, Sweep, Register;
- artifact: Frontier, Ash;
- no-S null;
- Scout absorbed into Trace;
- Stress-test and Simulate incompletely contracted.

It also found that current Skaffen does not justify a seventh runtime phase, material epistemic separation is safer than annotations, subtraction/retirement is the clearest capability gap, and current pilot instrumentation is confounded. Treat these as claims to verify or bound, not axioms.

## Required completion work

1. Verify or refute the fourteen raw round-3 findings `f-060` through `f-073`, prioritizing `f-063`, `f-064`, `f-066`, and `f-070`.
2. Repair the balanced field just enough to compare it:
   - give Stress-test its strongest non-redundant contract, or explain why it dissolves into Reflect/Assay;
   - decide whether Simulate is distinct from Sweep, or cluster them;
   - do not introduce unrelated candidates.
3. Apply the hard gates from the v2 charter. A candidate failing phasehood may survive as a side-loop, capability, policy, or artifact.
4. Rank the five main policy claimants—Fallow, Stint, Slacken, Season, Divert—against each other and no-S. Survey may be paired with Fallow if the 2×2 identification claim survives verification.
5. Compare the strongest survivor from each class, rather than allowing a long class to dominate by candidate count.
6. Provide the missing qualitative matrix with uncertainty, steelman/attack, conditional recommendation, and reversal conditions.
7. Exercise the neglected dimensions explicitly: mnemonic/taste, cognitive cost, human operability, and multi-agent/topology interaction.
8. Produce minimal pilots for the top two actionable designs and the no-S null using identical task buckets, stores, budgets, and compaction treatment.

## Decision distinctions

Report three separate verdicts:

- **Does any candidate earn a distinct semantic operation?**
- **Does any candidate earn a distinct runtime phase now?**
- **What capability/policy/artifact should actually be implemented first?**

Do not force the same winner across these questions.

## Comparison criteria

Use qualitative ratings with explicit uncertainty:

- semantic distinctness from O/O/D/A/R/C;
- epistemic safety and material separation;
- generativity and validated discovery;
- falsification/calibration;
- pace/shear fit;
- enforceability in an agent harness;
- cognitive and implementation cost;
- mnemonic/taste;
- multi-agent/topology coherence;
- marginal value over no-S.

A recommendation must state what evidence would reverse it.

## Adversarial obligations

For every shortlisted option provide:

- strongest steelman;
- strongest attack;
- failure/Goodhart mode;
- losing pilot outcome;
- whether another existing phase or candidate carries the same load.

Explicitly test these propositions:

1. **Scout/Trace:** useful exploration contract but not a phase.
2. **Stress-test/Assay:** falsification may be more distinct and safer than generation.
3. **Reprove+Melt:** subtractive maintenance may be the real missing operation.
4. **Fallow(+Survey):** measure the incumbent before adding machinery.
5. **Stint/Season/Slacken/Divert:** allocation policy may dominate artifact-emitting S candidates.
6. **No-S:** existing Orient/Reflect/Compound plus explicit tools may suffice.

## Required output

1. Verified disposition of `f-060..f-073`.
2. Final shortlist grouped by semantic operation/runtime phase/policy/capability/artifact.
3. Qualitative comparison matrix with uncertainty.
4. Steelman and attack for every shortlist member.
5. Policy-class ranking.
6. Verdicts for semantic S, runtime S, and first implementation.
7. Top-two plus null pilots.
8. Residual human judgments.
9. Confidence and reversal conditions.

The desired answer may be conditional. Prefer the smallest coherent architecture over preserving the acronym.
