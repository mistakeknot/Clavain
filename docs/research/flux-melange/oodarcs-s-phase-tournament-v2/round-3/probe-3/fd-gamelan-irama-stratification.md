## Findings

### warrant-elided-before-claim

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** `internal/agentloop/autocompact.go:135-176` (`microCompact`), esp. `:149` and `:157`; applied every turn at `internal/agentloop/loop.go:145-181`; target `:99` (epistemic separation gate), `:82` (field 4).
- **What:** The tournament is grading candidates on whether they *name* a grade, a namespace, a read filter and an expiry. Run the metadata-strip test and the strip is not a thought experiment — Skaffen performs it on a timer. `microCompact` walks every message outside the `KeepRecent: 6` tail and replaces the content of any `tool_result` block over 200 chars with `[output truncated — N chars]` (`:149`, `:157`). It touches nothing else. Assistant `text` blocks — the prose in which a probe states what it found, how confident it is, and which grade it carries — are never stubbed and never age out until `snip` deletes the whole message (`:194-215`). So a long session monotonically converts its context from *evidence + interpretation* to *interpretation alone*: the grep output that warranted the analogy is a stub while the sentence "the mechanism transfers" survives verbatim, indistinguishable in register from a validated finding. The consumer here is the next turn's model, and it reads no metadata: `tool_result` and assistant `text` arrive in the same `messages` slice with no grade field on either.
- **Evidence:** `autocompact.go:149` — `if block.Type == "tool_result" && len(block.ResultContent) > 200` is the *only* branch that elides content; the stub at `:157` preserves `ToolUseID` and `IsError` but drops the payload, and no compensating write is made anywhere (`internal/agent/deps.go:61-86` shows `Evidence` records `ToolCalls []string` — names only — and `FileActivity`, never result content). `ReplaceMessages` (`internal/session/session.go:417-422`) is in-memory only, so nothing downstream can recover the elided warrant either.
- **Suggestion:** Hard-gate consequence first: **no candidate may satisfy gate 3 with an in-band annotation.** Separation must be carried by material — a distinct store, a distinct tool, or a distinct message role — because the only in-band carrier Skaffen has (tool_result content) is the one thing the runtime deletes first. Smallest viable diff if in-band carriage is kept anyway: make the stub self-warranting by naming its source and its store key rather than only its length — `fmt.Sprintf("[evidence elided — %s — %d chars — probe %s]", block.Name, oldLen, ref)` at `autocompact.go:157`, with `ref` written to the evidence dir at elision time. One hunk, and it converts a silent strip into a citation the next turn can re-fetch.

### pilot-has-no-clock-only-arm

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** target `:142-157` (pilot contract), `:71` (the "changes exploration/exploitation policy rather than emitting a report" slot); `internal/agent/agent.go:227-229`; `internal/agentloop/loop.go:135`, `:145-181`; `internal/agentloop/autocompact.go:53-64`.
- **What:** Two problems, one fix. (a) The charter's arms are baseline, candidate A, candidate B — three arms that differ in what they *emit*. Nothing in the design holds the loop's clock constant across them, and the clock is not a constant: `calculateTokenPressure` fires compaction on `tokenCount >= effectiveWindow - 13000` (`autocompact.go:57`, `:101-127`). At equal token budget, the arm that explores more distinct material reaches that threshold sooner and therefore gets snipped more often, so "equal-budget traces" are not equal-clock traces. The blind-scored metric *disconfirmed hypotheses retained* (`:152`) is then anti-correlated with the treatment by construction: more probing → earlier `snip` → fewer retained disconfirmations, with no arm-level record of the deletion. (b) The charter demands a candidate that changes exploration/exploitation policy rather than emitting a report (`:71`) and the field has not filed one that survives, because such a candidate looks empty. It is not empty in Skaffen — it is the only candidate class with an enforcement seat that is currently welded shut.
- **Evidence:** `agent.go:227-229` builds `DefaultAutoCompactConfig()` once per `Run` and hands it to the loop; `loop.go:135` reads `l.maxTurns` fixed at construction (`loop.go:92`, default 100; `cmd/skaffen/main.go:57` flag, `session.New(..., 20)` at `main.go:312` and `:548` for the retention window). No caller — no phase, no tool, no router — can change `KeepRecent`, `BufferTokens`, or the turn allowance mid-run. Compare the settled verdict that Decide has *no* enforceable delta of any kind (`internal/tool/registry.go:49-58`, `internal/router/router.go:20-27`): the pace-layer operation has a concrete one and no incumbent leg does.
- **Suggestion:** File **Slacken** and run it as arm C. Contract, eleven fields, compressed: (1) *in* — current phase, `TokenPressure`, remaining allowance; precondition: called only at a phase boundary. (2) *transformation* — changes the loop's base clock for a pre-declared span: raises `AutoCompactConfig.KeepRecent`/`BufferTokens` and the phase's turn allowance, then restores. No content transformation whatsoever. (3) *output* — none; the consumer is the loop. (4) *grade/store/filter/expiry* — vacuous by construction: it creates no speculative material, so there is nothing to separate, nothing to expire, and nothing to launder. It is the only candidate in the field that passes hard gate 3 without a store. (5) *trigger/pace* — announced at a phase boundary with one turn of lead-in; pace layer = the run, not the turn. (6) *re-entry* — fixed before launch: the same phase, at the pre-declared turn, landing on the existing `KeepRecent: 6` tail. (7) *delta* — make `acCfg` and the turn allowance per-phase mutable (`agent.go:227-229`); this is the whole implementation. (8) *overlap* — none; it emits no product, so it cannot duplicate Orient or Compound. (9) *failure/Goodhart* — called to buy context rather than exploration; bound it by requiring the span to be declared before the widening takes effect. (10) *pilot* — arm C is arm A with `KeepRecent: 24` and `BufferTokens` doubled and nothing else changed. **If arm C matches arm A or B on validated discoveries, every report-emitting candidate is dominated by a config change and the no-S null wins on cost.** (11) *classification* — not a phase: a routing/pace modifier, i.e. a strengthening of the no-S null. Additionally, predeclare and log the compaction schedule per arm (`snip` count, `microCompact` bytes elided) as a covariate; an arm's discovery score is uninterpretable without it.

### survival-cluster-collapses-the-emitters

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** `internal/agent/agent.go:420-499` (`buildPostCompactHook`), esp. the tool whitelist at `:452-453` and the restoration text at `:466-490`; `internal/agentloop/autocompact.go:194-215` (`snip`); `internal/session/session.go:337-348` (`truncate`); target `:41` (lesson 5), `:124` (Scout vs Speculate).
- **What:** The charter clusters candidates by contract; cluster them instead by *what persists past the next structural boundary* and the field shrinks hard. In Skaffen the boundary is auto-compaction, and exactly three things cross it: the first 200 characters of the first user message, up to ten file paths harvested from `read`/`write`/`edit` tool calls, and the phase string (`agent.go:423-439`, `:452-453`, `:466-490`). That is the skeleton. Everything else — every grep, every glob, every web fetch, every speculative report the assistant wrote — is either stubbed by `microCompact` or deleted outright by `snip`, which replaces the entire history with one user-role marker and the last six messages (`autocompact.go:200-213`). Consequences the tournament has not priced: (i) **Scout, Speculate, Synthesize and Trace have identical survival residue — zero — so under this test they are one operation, not four.** Their contracts differ only upstream of the gong. (ii) Lesson 5 ("do not erase failed probes") is violated by the runtime before any candidate can obey it: a probe that fails is exactly the material `snip` reaches first, and the deletion emits nothing to the evidence stream (`internal/agent/deps.go:61-86` has no field for it). The raw turn is appended to `<session>.jsonl` (`session.go:18-25`, `appendRecord`), but no consumer ever reads that tape except an explicit `-r` resume, which itself calls `truncate()` and keeps only `maxTurns*2` messages (`session.go:293`, `:337-348`). Retention on a tape nobody reads is not retention. (iii) The restoration message is emitted with `provider.RoleUser` (`agent.go:492-498`), the highest-trust register in the transcript — so whatever *does* survive is promoted from assistant speculation to user-voice premise on the way through.
- **Evidence:** `agent.go:452` — `case "read", "write", "edit", "Read", "Write", "Edit":` is the complete set of tools whose activity survives compaction. Search tools contribute nothing. Cross-check against the settled finding that Compound's only durable outputs are a rates-only `QualitySignal` and a manifest-globbed markdown write: neither is reachable from mid-phase, so there is no in-run stock for an S to deposit into.
- **Suggestion:** Two moves. (1) Adopt the survival test as a clustering rule in Required output item 1 (`:161`): two candidates that persist the same thing in the same way are one candidate, whatever their verbs. This eliminates the Scout/Speculate/Synthesize distinction without further argument and leaves the field to the candidates that change a *stock* (Assay, Reprove, Slacken). (2) File **Ash**, the unretained-elaboration contract, as the shape the runtime already wants: its speculative text is designed to die at the gong, and the only thing it is allowed to persist is one line of changed stock. Smallest viable delta — extend the whitelist at `agent.go:452-453` to a `Probes:` line sourced from that stock and rendered in the restoration text at `:466-490`, one hunk plus a store write. Until such a stock exists, every contract in the field that answers field 4 with "retained for the rest of the session" is answering about a store Skaffen does not have.

### positional-admissibility-has-no-wire

- **Severity:** P2
- **Where:** `internal/agentloop/loop.go:189-195` (`PromptHints{Budget, Model, PlanMode, TurnCount}`); `internal/agent/agent.go:345-353` (`sessionAdapter.SystemPrompt` forwards only `hints.Budget`); `internal/session/session.go:78` (`func (s *JSONLSession) SystemPrompt(phase tool.Phase, _ int) string` — the parameter is discarded); target `:82` (read filter), `:84` (pace layer).
- **What:** Several contracts in the field answer field 4's read filter with age or grade ("expires after N days", "grade C is not evidence"). There is a second, cheaper filter this domain uses instead: material is admissible at some positions in the cycle and inadmissible at others, regardless of how old or how good it is. Speculative transfer material is admissible while the phase is still opening and inadmissible once the loop has committed to an action — not because it decayed, but because of where the loop is standing. Skaffen computes exactly the two quantities such a filter needs, every single turn, and throws both away before the prompt is assembled: the loop passes `TurnCount` and `Budget` in `PromptHints` (`loop.go:189-195`); the adapter forwards only `Budget` (`agent.go:349`); the session signature discards it as `_ int` (`session.go:78`). Every phase-keyed injection therefore reads identically at turn 1 and turn 40.
- **Evidence:** `session.go:78-101` gates injections on `phase` alone — Orient gets quality history and inspiration, Act gets fault-localization guidance, Reflect gets its guidance. No injection can be made position-sensitive without changing the signature, because the position never arrives. Note this is a *filter* gap, not a provenance gap: the grading and expiry questions belong to the assay and warrant contracts and are settled elsewhere.
- **Suggestion:** File **Register**: an S whose read filter is keyed to loop position rather than material age — its output is admissible into Orient's prompt only while `TurnCount` is inside the phase's opening window, and becomes inadmissible at the first Act regardless of its grade or age. It needs no new store and no new grade schema, which is precisely why it is cheap. Delta: change `agent.Session.SystemPrompt(phase tool.Phase, budget int)` to take the `PromptHints` struct (or add `turn int`) and forward `hints.TurnCount` at `agent.go:349` — a signature change plus one call site, after which a position-keyed filter is a two-line `if` in `session.go:80-92`. Question for the field rather than an assertion: does any surviving contract's read filter actually need age, or would position do the same work with no expiry machinery at all?

## Verdict

Read from the gong backwards, Skaffen already is a stratified ensemble: dense free elaboration between boundaries, and a skeleton of goal, files and phase that alone survives — so the tournament's report-emitting candidates all persist the same thing, which is nothing, and collapse into one cluster. The runtime performs the metadata-strip test on a timer, eliding each probe's warrant while keeping its claim, which disqualifies any candidate that answers hard gate 3 with an in-band annotation. The pace-layer slot the charter left open (`:71`) is the only candidate class with a live enforcement seat, and until a clock-only arm runs beside arms A and B, the pilot cannot tell a new operation from a wider `KeepRecent`.
