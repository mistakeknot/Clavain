- [P2] retention-keyed-by-tasktype-not-source-patch — failed probes are retained under `ClassifyTask`'s verb buckets, so a negative transfer never lowers the sampling weight of the domain that produced it (§Lessons item 5 (41), §Per-candidate contract 82)

## Findings

### no-exchange-rate-probes-per-punch
- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** `docs/research/2026-08-24-oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2.md` §Per-candidate contract item 4 (line 82) and §Hard gates (line 101); `internal/mutations/aggregate.go:28,70-72`; `internal/mutations/inspire.go:20-40,62-85`; `internal/session/session.go:80-92`; `internal/tool/registry.go:65,243-258`
- **What:** The charter gates a candidate on *having* a bounded stop rule (line 101) and, separately, on *having* an authority grade and read filter (line 82). It never asks a candidate to state the exchange rate between them: how many probes may be emitted per unit of grading capacity, and what the loop does when that ratio is violated. In Skaffen the grading capacity is a hard, known constant — `Aggregate` produces exactly one `QualitySignal` per session (`aggregate.go:28`), so the office punches one mark per session no matter how many speculative artifacts the S emitted. Probe production is O(k) per session; punching is O(1).
- **Evidence:** The stock does not sit inert; it is read back. `JSONLSession.SystemPrompt` injects `mutations.FormatInspiration(...)` into the Orient system prompt (`session.go:86-91`), and `Store.Inspire` composes that from three sources, the third being `cassSearch` (`inspire.go:35,62-85`) — a shell-out to `cass search` over prior session transcripts that is grade-blind by construction: it has no authority field, no expiry, and no way to distinguish a validated finding from a speculative report the S wrote three sessions ago. Concrete failure: an S wins the tournament, emits ~5 speculative transfer reports per session; Compound punches one aggregate mark; after ten sessions ~50 unmarked reports are recoverable by `cass search` and land verbatim in the next Orient prompt. Orient then optimizes against a corpus whose median item was never assayed — a competing truth channel created by throughput, not by a missing read filter (the read filter would be satisfied on paper by the store boundary in field 4).
- **Suggestion:** Add a twelfth contract field, or fold it into field 4: **probes per punch** — the maximum ungraded artifacts the candidate may hold outstanding, and the loop's behavior on breach (block emission, or auto-melt oldest). Skaffen already has the enforcement primitive: `GateConstraint.RateLimit` (`registry.go:243-258`), used today only for Reflect's `edit` (`registry.go:65`). A winning S's emit tool should carry `&GateConstraint{RateLimit: N}` where N is the per-session grading quota, and `cassSearch` results must be tagged before injection or excluded from Orient.

### discovery-count-not-charged-the-assay
- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** §Pilot contract (lines 142, 148-157)
- **What:** The arms are equalized in turns/tokens ("Compare equal-budget traces", line 142), but the winning metric — "validated mechanism discoveries" (line 149) — is struck by a blind scorer *after* the trace ends. Validation is therefore free to the arm that generated the lead. The charter charges false-transfer cost and time-to-useful-probe (lines 151-152) but never charges the cost of the assay that turned a lead into a *validated* discovery.
- **Evidence:** Nothing in the runtime meters grading effort: the only currencies `Aggregate` can compute are `TokenEfficiency` and `TurnCount` over turn records (`aggregate.go:62-72`). So "equal budget" is enforceable only on the generative half of the loop. Failure scenario: candidate A spends its whole budget emitting twelve cheap touchstone-grade analogies and validates none in-loop; the blind scorer, working off the pre-registered corpus's hidden mechanisms, marks four of them "validated"; candidate B spends the same budget cupelling two leads to dispositive grade and scores two. A wins by 4-2 while having consumed four units of an office that has no budget line and, at scale, no staff. The tournament then promotes the arm that externalizes its assay cost.
- **Suggestion:** In the pilot contract, split the metric by declared tier and charge the office: report *screened leads per turn* and *dispositive validations per turn* separately, and require the blind scorer to log the turns/tokens it spent validating each arm's output, reported alongside the arm's own budget. A candidate wins only if it improves dispositive validations per **total** (arm + office) unit.
- **Remediation:** Amend §Pilot contract to require that the blind scorer's own validation effort be metered per arm and added to that arm's budget before any discovery count is compared.

### equivalence-key-cannot-separate-divert-from-null
- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** §Lessons item 6 (line 42), §Candidate-generation requirement (lines 71-73)
- **What:** Lesson 6 fixes the contract-equivalence key as "contracts, triggers, outputs, or authority", and line 73 forbids scoring before clustering on that key. Every term in that key is output-shaped. A candidate whose entire transformation is a change to the exploration/exploitation policy — which line 71 *mandates* the field contain — emits no artifact, carries no authority grade, and needs no read filter. Under the stated key it is indistinguishable from the no-S null, which also emits nothing. The one pair the field most needs separated ("reallocate attention" vs "do nothing") is precisely the pair the key cannot resolve.
- **Evidence:** The allocation-side machinery Skaffen already has is entirely gradeless and artifact-free: `complexityTracker.shouldEscalate` returns `(bool, string)` from turn counts, unique-file counts and consecutive failures (`internal/costrouter/complexity.go:98-127`), and `phaseDefaults` maps every OODARC phase to the same model (`internal/router/router.go:20-27`). A policy-changing S is a delta to exactly these — no store, no expiry, no read filter. Run the charter's clustering literally and it either drops out of the longlist for failing to populate contract field 4, or clusters with no-S. Either way the twelve-candidate field is scored short one of its two mandated exotic entries.
- **Suggestion:** Extend the equivalence key with two non-output dimensions before clustering: **probe tier** (indicative screen vs dispositive test) and **allocation delta** (what changes in the sampling/routing policy when the candidate is on vs off). Divert's key becomes `tier=none, allocation-delta=escalation-threshold`; the null's becomes `tier=none, allocation-delta=∅` — distinct. This also stops Trace and Assay collapsing together, since they differ on tier and on whether the mark is struck on material the operation did not produce.
- **Remediation:** Amend §Lessons item 6 to state the contract-equivalence key as contract, trigger, output, authority, **probe tier, and exploration/exploitation allocation delta**, so candidates that emit no artifact remain distinguishable from the null.

### retention-keyed-by-tasktype-not-source-patch
- **Severity:** P2
- **Where:** §Lessons item 5 (line 41), §Per-candidate contract item 4 (line 82); `internal/mutations/inspire.go:43-59`; `internal/mutations/store.go:89-92,113,134-138`
- **What:** Lesson 5 justifies retention on a base-rate claim — negative transfer results are "durable evidence about source-domain base rates". The contract field, however, only asks *that* failed probes be retained, not that they be keyed by the thing the base rate is about. Retained-but-unkeyed is narrative retention: the record exists and changes nothing.
- **Evidence:** Skaffen's store has no source-domain dimension at all. The only index is `TaskType` (`store.go:113,134-138` via `taskTypeFile`), and `ClassifyTask` derives it by substring-matching the *task description* for "fix", "refactor", "optimize", "doc", "add" (`inspire.go:43-59`). A failed transfer borrowed from, say, immunology is filed under `feature`; the next probe into immunology is drawn with an unchanged prior, and `BestSummary`/`Suggest` (`inspire.go:25-33`) surface nothing about the barren patch. The same source domain is re-probed indefinitely, and each re-probe still costs a full assay.
- **Suggestion:** Require field 4 to name the **retention key** as the source domain/patch, not the session or task, and to state the effect on future allocation (e.g. a per-domain failure count that down-weights sampling). Smallest concrete fix in-repo: add a `SourceDomain string` to `QualitySignal` (`internal/mutations/signal.go:17-25`) and a per-domain tally consulted in `Store.Inspire` alongside `BestSummary`.

## Verdict
The tournament's machinery is output-shaped in three places where it needs to be allocation-shaped, and the fusion is where that bites: the contract counts artifacts but never states how many probes one punch buys, the pilot counts discoveries in a currency the generating arm never spends, and the equivalence key cannot tell a policy change from doing nothing. Fix the exchange-rate field first — it is the only one that, left alone, lets the winning S manufacture a competing truth channel out of pure throughput while satisfying every hard gate on paper.
