## Findings

### fallow-is-the-instrument-not-a-claimant
- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** `docs/research/2026-08-24-oodarcs-s-phase-ranking-charter.md:36` (disputed location), with `:54`
- **What:** Adjudicating the two prior findings: **(2) the dial claim holds and dominates; (1) the underdetermination claim is real but strictly subordinate, and fixing it first makes the defect worse.** This is not an irreducible taste call — it is decidable on the filed contracts.

  Checked against the source ledger the charter's frontmatter names, the five rows are not five designs. Four are settings of one control variable (the exploration quota) differing in *entry rule*, and one is a different control surface entirely:

  | row | filed control | setting |
  |---|---|---|
  | Fallow (f-063) | S-tasking quota | **zero** — "Sets S-tasking quota to zero; nothing else changes" |
  | Season (f-057) | S-tasking quota | fixed cohort at a pre-committed epoch rate, **no trigger** |
  | Stint (f-067) | allocation ratio | adaptive Strike record, **shear-only trigger**, dwell + hysteresis band |
  | Divert (f-034) | probe budget | reallocates next k turns on a **stagnation** signal, charged per-lead |
  | Slacken (f-071) | context/compaction clock | `KeepRecent` / `BufferTokens` — **not a quota at all** |

  So the dial claim is stronger than filed (four rows, not three), and its sharpest form is not "a dial is ranked against its zero position" but: **Fallow is the instrumentation of the no-S null, and requirement 4 enters it into the field as a rival to the null it measures.** f-063's own claim says so — it supplies "the zero point that the affirmative no-S null (:43) and the equal-budget pilot (:142) both presuppose and neither measures." Running Fallow *is* running no-S with a counter attached.

  Why (1) is subordinate rather than co-equal: the settled facts already establish that every value criterion at `:54-65` is a declared tie (no candidate has a single observation) and only cost-to-instrument can be ordered. Fallow is by construction the cheapest — "countable today from the existing evidence record's `tool_calls` (aggregate.go:14-24) with **zero new instrumentation**." The ranking's outcome is therefore foreordained before any rating vocabulary is chosen. Supplying the missing vocabulary, combination rule and tie-break that finding (1) asks for does not repair this; it **launders** it, converting "the off-position of the dial is cheapest to instrument" into a crisply-specified total order that reads "Fallow beat Stint, Season, Slacken and Divert on ten criteria" — and that laundered result then feeds requirement 8's "top two actionable designs" and the first-implementation verdict at `:48`. Precision applied to a malformed object removes the visible softness that would otherwise have flagged the result as non-load-bearing.
- **Evidence:** Target `:36` ("Rank the five main policy claimants—Fallow, Stint, Slacken, Season, Divert—against each other and no-S"), `:54`. Ledger `flux-melange/oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2/heat-ledger.jsonl`: f-063 field 2 and field 3; f-057 field 5 ("no trigger — a pre-committed rate on the slow layer"); f-067 field 5 ("trigger = shear only; dwell floor denominated in completed Compound writes"); f-034 ("reallocates the next k turns from exploitation to probing on a stagnation signal"); f-071 (clock-only). Synthesis `2026-08-24-synthesis.md:457-460` concedes the claimants are "not contract-equivalent" but distinguishes them **only by entry rule** — which is exactly the dial-setting reading — and `:480-486` (D6) leaves the fixed-vs-adaptive shape question open, i.e. the real live question is which control law, not which candidate.
- **Suggestion:** Restructure requirement 4 into two artifacts. (a) A **baseline decision**: run Fallow or not — it is the measurement of the null, so it is scheduled, not ranked, and its losing condition (f-063 field 10: spontaneous exploration already meets the proposed quota) is the tournament's kill switch, not a ranking row. (b) A **control-law comparison** over the three genuine entry rules — untriggered fixed rate (Season), shear-triggered adaptive with dwell (Stint), stagnation-triggered per-lead reallocation (Divert) — decided by argument on the D6 identification question (only a fixed rate makes a doubled yield distinguish a richer patch from a looser licence), not by rating five incommensurable rows. Reduced to three commensurable rows with one live discriminator, finding (1)'s vocabulary gap becomes tractable rather than load-bearing.
- **Remediation (target amendment):** Amend requirement 4 to strike Fallow from the ranked field and redesignate it as the instrumentation of the no-S null, ranking only the three entry-rule control laws (Season's untriggered fixed rate, Stint's shear-triggered adaptive strike, Divert's stagnation-triggered per-lead reallocation) against that measured baseline.

### slacken-is-a-pilot-control-req-8-forbids
- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** `docs/research/2026-08-24-oodarcs-s-phase-ranking-charter.md:36` against `:40`
- **What:** New insight the contradiction exposes. Once the four quota rows collapse into one dial, the fifth row stops looking like a weak claimant and starts looking misfiled. Slacken is the **only** claimant in the policy class whose contract is grounded in a verified enforcement point in Skaffen — f-071: `acCfg` is built once per Run (agent.go:227-229) and `maxTurns` is fixed at construction, so "the pace-layer candidate has a real enforcement seat that Decide entirely lacks." Under the settled fact that the re-imported hard gate ranks candidates in inverse order of governability, and under a ranking that will resolve on cost-to-instrument alone, the one governable candidate is eliminated by a metric orthogonal to its claim.

  Worse, f-071 never filed Slacken as a candidate. It filed it as a **required pilot covariate control**: `calculateTokenPressure` fires at `tokenCount >= effectiveWindow - BufferTokens` (autocompact.go:57), so the arm that explores more hits compaction *sooner* and gets snipped more — deleting exactly the retained-disconfirmation material the pilot blind-scores. Its prescription is "arm C = arm A with KeepRecent 24 and BufferTokens doubled, nothing else changed."

  That collides head-on with requirement 8. `:40` mandates pilots "using identical task buckets, stores, budgets, and **compaction treatment**." Identical compaction *configuration* is precisely what f-071 shows does not produce an identical *clock* — and the required control arm is one that deliberately differs in compaction settings. So the charter simultaneously (a) competes Slacken out of the field at `:36` and (b) forbids at `:40` the very arm Slacken exists to supply, while depending on that arm for the pilots to be interpretable at all.
- **Evidence:** Target `:36`, `:40`. Ledger f-071 claim and evidence (autocompact.go:57 threshold; agent.go:227-229; loop.go:92,:135; cmd/skaffen/main.go:57,:312,:548). Synthesis `:187`. Note the interaction with the settled fact that the Compound-gated evidence write means conditions about abandoned exploration read on nothing: compaction-driven deletion is a second, independent path by which the pilot loses the disconfirmation signal it is supposed to score.
- **Suggestion:** Move Slacken out of the policy-claimant field entirely and into the pilot design as a mandatory clock-only control arm; log snip count and `microCompact` bytes elided per arm as a predeclared covariate, so "equal budget" claims are checkable rather than asserted. Requirement 4 then ranks three control laws, requirement 8 runs four arms (top-two + null + clock-only control), and Slacken's enforcement-seat advantage is used where it is real rather than scored where it is irrelevant.
- **Remediation (target amendment):** Amend requirement 8 to replace "identical … compaction treatment" with a mandatory clock-only control arm (KeepRecent and BufferTokens varied, nothing else changed, snip count and elided bytes logged per arm), and relocate Slacken from requirement 4's candidate field into that control role.

## Verdict
The dial claim wins and the underdetermination claim is subordinate: requirement 4's field contains four settings of one exploration-quota dial — including the null's own measuring instrument — plus one row that is not a quota candidate at all, and since every value criterion is a declared tie, supplying the missing rating vocabulary would only launder a cost-to-instrument tautology into a five-way verdict that feeds the first-implementation decision. Not a taste call: the filed contracts (f-063 field 2, f-057 field 5, f-067 field 5, f-034, f-071) decide it. Split requirement 4 into a baseline decision (Fallow, scheduled not ranked) and a three-way control-law comparison, and relocate Slacken into requirement 8 as the clock-only control arm that `:40`'s "identical compaction treatment" currently forbids.
