### register-is-a-rotation-not-a-capability

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** charter :20 (capability class lists Register); :30 (f-073 is inside the f-060..f-073 verification set); source finding f-073 in `docs/research/flux-melange/oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2/heat-ledger.jsonl`
- **What:** Register is filed as a capability candidate, but its place notation is empty. Verified on the trace: `internal/agentloop/loop.go:189-195` already computes and passes `TurnCount: turn` every iteration; `internal/agent/agent.go:345-349` (`sessionAdapter.SystemPrompt`) forwards only `hints.Budget`; `internal/session/session.go:78` is `func (s *JSONLSession) SystemPrompt(phase tool.Phase, _ int) string`. Register's entire proposal is to stop discarding the second argument and put a two-line `if` around the existing Orient injections at `session.go:79-90`. The material read is identical (`formatQualityHistory` + `Inspire`), the producer is identical, the consumer is identical. The only change is *when the same work is admitted* — which is the definition of a rotation, not a method and not a call. It rings the row Orient already rings, one blow earlier or later.
- **Evidence:** `session.go:78` signature verified verbatim (`_ int`); `loop.go:189-195` verified passing `TurnCount`; `agent.go:349` verified forwarding `hints.Budget` only. The synthesis already lists this same plumbing under "independently actionable runtime bugs ... discarded `TurnCount` (f-066/f-073)" — i.e. the run itself classified the underlying fact as a bug, then f-073 re-filed it as a candidate.
- **Failure scenario:** Register survives to the shortlist as the cheapest capability. Requirement 5 (:37) then compares "strongest survivor per class" and requirement :48 asks what to implement first. Register wins "first implementation" on cost, and a two-line argument-threading bug fix ships under the S banner, consuming the S decision and producing no operation. Whoever reads the verdict later believes an S capability was piloted.
- **Suggestion:** In the disposition table, mark Register **rotation → refused at the semantic-operation gate**, and re-file the `TurnCount` plumbing as a repo bug alongside dead `ResetRateCounts` and unwritable `optimization.jsonl`. If a positional read filter is still wanted after the fix, it re-enters as a *setting* of the Orient injection, not as a class member.

### five-policy-claimants-are-three-calls

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** charter :36 ("Rank the five main policy claimants—Fallow, Stint, Slacken, Season, Divert—against each other and no-S")
- **What:** The charter treats five claimants as five distinct calls. Ask the call-vocabulary question — *what does each do to the coursing order that the others do not?* — and three of them collapse. Fallow (f-063) sets the S-tasking quota to zero for a pre-registered window. Stint (f-067) sets a probe:grade ratio by an adaptive strike rule. Season (f-057) sets a probe rate by a pre-committed cohort rule. All three pull the same lever (the exploration quota); they differ only in the *value* and in the *rule that chooses the value*. Fallow's celebrated product — one number, the incumbent's spontaneous rate — is a *consequence* of setting the dial to zero, not a second operation. In the tower this is one call ("Stint") with a conductor's rule and a zero setting, not three calls. The genuinely distinct calls in the policy class are: **quota** (Fallow/Stint/Season), **clock** (Slacken), **sampling frame** (Survey), **redirection on stagnation** (Divert), **intensity** (Widen).
- **Evidence:** f-063 field (2) is "Sets S-tasking quota to zero; nothing else changes"; f-067 field (1)/(3) is a `Strike` record `{ratio, struck_at, dwell_until, band}` written to `strike.jsonl`; f-057 is "a fixed-size cohort of exploration warrants minted at a pre-committed epoch." The synthesis registers all three as *mutually exclusive claimants* for one slot (D3, D6) — that mutual exclusivity is itself the tell that they are settings of a single lever, since two genuinely different calls can be rung in the same touch.
- **Failure scenario:** Requirement 4 produces a five-row ranking. Fallow ranks first (it is the cheapest and needs no new store). A reader takes that as "the quota-policy question is decided in favour of withholding," when what was actually decided is "measure before you set the dial" — a sequencing claim, not a winner. Stint and Season then get implemented anyway six months later, unranked against each other, because the ranking never actually compared the two *rules*.
- **Suggestion:** Restructure requirement 4's output as: one policy call (quota) with three settings ranked *as settings* — zero (Fallow) first as the mandatory plain course, then the adaptive-vs-pre-committed rule question (Stint vs Season) declared **unresolved** and carried as the single open policy question — plus three separate calls (Slacken, Divert, Widen) ranked against the null on their own. Survey stays separate on the f-064 2×2, which survives this test because it changes the *frame*, not the quota.

### slacken-has-no-conductor-seat

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** charter :36 (Slacken as a policy claimant), :40 (pilots must hold "compaction treatment" identical); source finding f-071
- **What:** f-071's strongest claim for Slacken is that "the pace-layer candidate has a real enforcement seat that Decide entirely lacks." Re-examined on the trace, the seat exists but is not reachable from inside the circle. `internal/agent/agent.go:227-229` builds `acCfg := agentloop.DefaultAutoCompactConfig()` once per `Run` and passes it as `agentloop.WithAutoCompact(acCfg)`; `BufferTokens: 13000` and `KeepRecent: 6` are constants in `DefaultAutoCompactConfig()` at `internal/agentloop/autocompact.go:55-62`; there is no setter, no phase hook, and no tool that mutates them mid-run. So Slacken can only ever be *rung before the band starts* — it is a different composition chosen at the launch, not a call a conductor makes on observing the loop. A call that cannot be made mid-touch is not in the call vocabulary.
- **Evidence:** `agent.go:227-229` verified; `autocompact.go:55-62` verified (`BufferTokens: 13000`, `KeepRecent: 6`); trigger arithmetic verified at `autocompact.go:106` (`threshold := effective - cfg.BufferTokens`). Grep finds no assignment to `acCfg.BufferTokens` or `.KeepRecent` outside construction.
- **Failure scenario:** Slacken is ranked as a policy candidate, wins on "has a real enforcement seat," and the implementer discovers the seat is a config literal — at which point Slacken either becomes a flag (no operation, no S) or requires an FSM/config-mutation change nobody scoped, and the policy ranking has to be redone.
- **Suggestion:** Disposition Slacken as **not a call — a composition parameter**, and split its two halves: the covariate control (arm C with doubled `BufferTokens`) belongs to the pilot design, not to the candidate field; the candidate claim survives only if field 7 names the concrete mid-run mutation path (a `SetAutoCompact` on the loop, or a per-phase `acCfg`).

### shortlist-costs-five-stores-one-blue-line

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** charter :39 ("Exercise the neglected dimensions explicitly: ... cognitive cost, human operability"), :62 ("cognitive and implementation cost")
- **What:** The charter asks for cognitive cost as a criterion but the field has never been priced in countable units. Counted: the surviving candidates propose five distinct destinations for S output — `quality-signals.jsonl` (incumbent), the Warrant allocation register (f-042/f-063), `strike.jsonl` (f-067), the Frontier lead ledger (f-032), and Ash (f-072). Practice-night accounting for the weakest operator: learning any single candidate is roughly one night (one trigger, one output, one read filter). Learning **which store a given S output lands in, and which of them Orient will read back** is three nights and never finishes, because there is no rule — each candidate names its own store in its own field 3. This is the field's dominant human cost and it is a *band* cost, not a candidate cost: it lands on every operator regardless of which candidate wins, and it is invisible to a per-candidate cost rating.
- **Evidence:** The incumbent has exactly one durable store and one read path — `Store` at `internal/mutations/store.go` (`signalFile = "quality-signals.jsonl"`, `dir` typically `~/.skaffen/mutations/`), read back only through `session.go:79-90` (`formatQualityHistory` + `Inspire`). Every candidate above adds a store *and* a read filter; none of them says what happens when two of them disagree about the same session.
- **Failure scenario:** Two candidates are implemented (the likely outcome of requirement :48 plus a follow-up). An operator debugging "why did Orient recommend that" now has to know which of three files was consulted and in what precedence — the same failure mode f-054/f-062 already produced *within a single store* (write key ≠ read key), reproduced across stores.
- **Suggestion:** Add a one-line band-effect column to the requirement-6 matrix: *destination store, and does Orient read it back?* — then rank shortlist members with a penalty for opening a new destination. The cheapest correct outcome is that every survivor writes to the existing store with a typed field, and only material that must be unreachable from `SystemPrompt` gets a new file (which f-070 says is exactly one class).

### s-name-premise-is-false-on-the-field

- **Severity:** P2 [taste]
- **Where:** charter :63 ("mnemonic/taste"); inherited from `docs/research/flux-melange/oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2/2026-08-24-synthesis.md:364`

Then decide D5 on the real question: whether the winning verb reads as one operation on the blue line (Reprove and Survey do; Register and Warrant do not).

## Verdict

Two dispositions carry the ranking: Register is a rotation and must be refused at the semantic gate, and the five "policy claimants" are three calls plus two settings of one quota lever — which means requirement 4's five-row ranking is the wrong shape before it is written. Slacken should be dispositioned out of the call vocabulary entirely unless field 7 names a mid-run mutation path. The human-cost dimension the charter flags as neglected has one dominant, countable term nobody has priced: five destination stores and no rule for which one a given output enters.
