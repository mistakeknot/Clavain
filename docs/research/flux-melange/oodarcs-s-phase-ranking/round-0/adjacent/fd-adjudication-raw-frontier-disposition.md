## Findings

### verification-standard-undefined

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** `docs/research/2026-08-24-oodarcs-s-phase-ranking-charter.md:30` ("Verify or refute the fourteen raw round-3 findings `f-060` through `f-073`") and `:90` ("Verified disposition of `f-060..f-073`").
- **What:** The charter demands a verified disposition but never states what verification requires, who may perform it, or what vocabulary a disposition is written in. Three sub-gaps: (a) no independence requirement, (b) no named artifact the check must run against, (c) no disposition vocabulary, so "verified" and "plausible" are the same word.
- **Evidence:** The source synthesis anticipates exactly this failure and the charter does not close it. `docs/research/flux-melange/oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2/2026-08-24-synthesis.md` Caveat 1: "Each carries a lens self-attestation ('VERIFIED on the trace' with file:line), and I spot-checked the file/line citations for internal consistency, but none received independent adjudication." The ledger confirms the state: every record from `f-060` to `f-073` in `heat-ledger.jsonl` carries `"status": "raw"`, and none carries a `lens` value (the field is null), so a downstream verifier cannot even tell which party filed the claim it is being asked to check. The only nearby discipline clause, `:26` ("Treat these as claims to verify or bound, not axioms"), governs the four inherited *conclusions*, not the finding-level check, and still names no standard. A run that re-reads `internal/agentloop/autocompact.go:149` because f-070 says to, finds the branch, and writes "confirmed" has performed the same check the filing lens already performed — the claim's *consequence* ("any candidate satisfying the epistemic-separation gate with an in-band grade fails it mechanically") is what needs adjudication, and nothing asks for it.
- **Suggestion:** One clause after `:30`: "Verification means an independent re-read of the cited file:line at current HEAD by a party that did not file the finding, plus an explicit ruling on the finding's *consequence*, recorded as exactly one of `confirmed` / `confirmed-narrowed` (state the narrower claim) / `refuted` / `unresolvable-without-a-run` (state the measurement). A lens's own 'VERIFIED on the trace' string is not evidence."

### refutation-dependency-undisclosed

- **Severity:** P1

- **What:** Four of the five policy claimants inherit a refuted or explicitly unrepaired premise, and the charter's per-candidate obligation list never requires that dependency be surfaced — so it will not appear in the shortlist, the matrix, or the ranking.
- **Evidence:** From the synthesis field table and §5/§7: Stint's origin is **f-067, which repairs the refuted f-052** — f-052's enforcement mechanism was `GateConstraint.RateLimit`, and that mechanism is broken in the running system: `ResetRateCounts` is documented "call on phase transition" at `internal/tool/registry.go:116` and has **zero production callers** (the only reference outside its definition is `internal/tool/registry_test.go:484`), so every `RateLimit` is a per-process-lifetime budget. Season's origin is **f-057**, whose field 7 asserts `ResetRateCounts` zeroes the counter on phase transition — the synthesis rules this "resolved against f-057" and states "its runtime-delta half must be re-derived before scoring." Fallow (f-063) is buildable only because f-004 was refuted (synthesis Caveat 8). Divert's origin is f-034 with f-001 refuted. **Failure scenario:** step 4 produces a five-way policy ranking in which Season is scored on "enforceability in an agent harness" (`:61`) using a field-7 mechanism the prior run already adjudicated non-functional; the downstream implementer builds a rate-limited cohort policy on a counter that never resets, and the pilot's "N probes per S visit" stop rule silently becomes N per process.
- **Suggestion:** Add a sixth bullet to §Adversarial obligations at `:77`: "which refuted or repaired findings the contract depends on, and whether the dependency has been re-derived — a candidate whose enforcement mechanism was adjudicated non-functional may not be scored on enforceability until field 7 is re-derived."

### raw-findings-rankable

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** `:30` (priority list `f-063`, `f-064`, `f-066`, `f-070`) against `:36` (rank the five policy claimants) and the §Required output ordering at `:90-98`.
- **What:** Two coupled defects. (a) The priority list omits the findings that actually carry three of the five policy claimants' contracts. (b) No clause forbids ranking or recommending a candidate whose origin finding is still `raw`, and required-output item 1 (verified disposition) can be satisfied *after* item 5 (policy-class ranking) without contradicting anything the charter says.
- **Evidence:** Ledger status is `raw` for all of `f-060..f-073`. The prioritized four cover Fallow (f-063), Survey (f-064), the allocator regress (f-066) and the compaction/annotation finding (f-070) — but **Stint's contract is f-067, Slacken's contract is f-071**, and the clustering rule the synthesis recommends adopting into required-output item 1 is **f-072** ("the survival test … Adopt it into Required-output item 1 alongside the existing contract-equivalence predicate"). All three are deprioritized by `:30`. So the charter's own step 4 ranks two claimants whose contracts sit outside its verification priority, using a shortlist rule (f-072) it never asked anyone to verify. **Failure scenario:** the run verifies the prioritized four, ranks Fallow first and Slacken second on f-071's compaction-covariate argument, and only afterward (or never) checks f-071 — which the ledger scores at likelihood 2, i.e. the least confident of the round-3 policy findings.
- **Suggestion:** Two small edits. At `:30`, extend the priority list to `f-067`, `f-071`, `f-072`. After `:34`, add: "No candidate may enter the shortlist, the matrix, or any ranking while its origin finding's disposition is `raw`, `refuted`, or `refuted-and-not-repaired`; a claimant whose origin finding is refuted at verification is struck from step 4 and the ranking proceeds over the remainder."

### no-discriminating-predicate-for-field-repair

- **Severity:** P2
- **Where:** `:32-33` ("give Stress-test its strongest non-redundant contract, or explain why it dissolves into Reflect/Assay; decide whether Simulate is distinct from Sweep, or cluster them").
- **What:** Both repairs are stated as decisions with no test attached, and the clustering key that would settle them is on record as incomplete. The answer is therefore settled by whichever framing the reviewer writes first, and is neither reproducible nor reversible.
- **Evidence:** Two usable predicates exist in the sources and the charter names neither. (1) The v2 charter's lesson 6 at `docs/research/2026-08-24-oodarcs-s-phase-tournament-v2.md:42`: "Cluster synonyms unless their contracts, triggers, outputs, or authority differ." (2) f-072's survival/persistence rule — "what crosses Skaffen's compaction boundary is only `{first 200 chars of goal, ≤10 read/write/edit paths, phase string}` … same persistence ⇒ same candidate" — which the synthesis §3 explicitly recommends adopting. Worse, the synthesis records the key as **known incomplete in two dimensions**: f-045 ("the clustering predicate merges on contract/trigger/output/authority but **never on spend**, so a cheap screen and a costly dispositive test collapse into one candidate before scoring") and f-059 (omits issuing authority). Simulate-vs-Sweep is precisely a spend distinction — Sweep reuses `internal/experiment`'s `ExpandMutations` capped at 24 permutations, which is a costly dispositive run, while a "Simulate" contract need not be. Under the unpatched key they merge; under a spend-aware key they may not. **Failure scenario:** the run clusters Simulate into Sweep, a later reader asks why, and no recorded predicate can be re-applied to check or reverse the call.
- **Suggestion:** Replace `:33` with: "Decide Simulate-vs-Sweep and Stress-test's disposition under a single stated predicate — contract/trigger/output/authority (v2 charter lesson 6) **extended with spend (f-045) and issuing authority (f-059)** — and record which key field separated or merged them."

## Verdict

The charter is unusually disciplined about *scope* — `:14` forbids restarting generation and re-auditing charter machinery, `:34` forbids unrelated candidates, and `:26` correctly refuses to promote the prior run's four conclusions to axioms — but it is silent on *epistemic procedure*, which is the half the prior run left unfinished. As written, "verified disposition of f-060..f-073" can be discharged by re-reading each finding's own self-attestation, and four of the five policy claimants can be ranked without ever disclosing that their contracts rest on refuted or unrepaired premises. Four small insertions — a verification definition with a disposition vocabulary, a refutation-dependency obligation, a raw-blocks-ranking rule, and a named clustering predicate extended with spend and authority — close the whole class without adding machinery.
