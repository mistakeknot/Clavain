# fd-usulfiqh-authority — round 0

Ladder audit taken before reading the candidates. The charter implies at least three grades — `speculative` (charter:38), "supported lesson" (charter:24), "validated lesson / durable knowledge" (charter:25) — and one raising act (a successful probe re-entering through Reflect, charter:41). For each rung: the act that reaches it is named only for the top rung, and **no act that leaves any rung is named anywhere**. In the runtime the ladder does not exist at all: `agent.Evidence` (`internal/agent/deps.go:61-96`) carries 25 fields and none of them is a grade, an authority, or a record identifier.

## Findings Index

- [P0] one-store-no-grade-four-hop-laundering — nothing in the charter enforces its own "cannot become evidence directly" rule, and the runtime has exactly one ungraded store whose aggregation feeds Orient (§Current leading candidate: Scout)
- [P1] expiry-names-no-actor-and-no-addressable-target — demotion is specified in neither direction nor location; the only durable store is append-only and its records have no id (§Current leading candidate: Scout)
- [P1] chain-severed-at-compound — a compounded lesson keeps no link to probe, report, or source domain, so discrediting a link can never trigger re-grading (§Required output)
- [P2] falsifiability-criterion-measures-probe-production-not-failure-attribution — a candidate scores full marks while its failed transfers leave no trace against the source domain (§Tournament criteria)
- [P2] analogy-validity-satisfiable-by-surface-resemblance — no shared effective cause is required and no bar on transferring from an ungraded source (§Current leading candidate: Scout)

## Findings

### one-store-no-grade-four-hop-laundering

- **Severity:** P0
- **Where:** charter:41 ("Scout reports cannot become evidence directly"), charter:75-76 (spec item 2, "Authority grade and storage boundary"), charter:91 (criterion "Epistemic safety")
- **What:** The rule is stated; nothing is named that enforces it, and the runtime offers no place to put it. There is one evidence store: `JSONLEmitter` appends every event to a single per-session file under `~/.skaffen/evidence/` (`internal/evidence/emitter.go:52-74`) and best-effort bridges the same event to intercore (`:44-48`). The record type has no grade field and no source field (`internal/agent/deps.go:61-96`). The laundering path is four hops and fully automatic: (1) any turn writes an ungraded record; (2) Compound calls `mutations.Aggregate(evidenceDir, sessionID)` (`internal/agent/agent.go:246-253`), which reads **every** line in the file and silently `continue`s past any record it cannot unmarshal (`internal/mutations/aggregate.go:36-47`) — so an unrecognised speculative shape is dropped rather than rejected, and a recognised one is counted; (3) the output `QualitySignal` retains only session id, timestamp, phase, task type, and aggregate rates (`internal/mutations/signal.go:17-46`) — every per-record distinction is gone; (4) a later Orient turn reads that signal back through `quality_history` (`internal/tool/quality_history.go:17-31`, gated to Orient at `internal/tool/builtin.go:26-29`). Citation scenario: six weeks after Scout ships, an Orient turn cites "recent sessions show a 0.31 tool error rate on refactor tasks" to justify a plan; part of that rate came from turns whose only purpose was probing a speculative cross-domain analogy, and there is no field anywhere in the chain that could have excluded them and no query that can find out. Every candidate that inherits this gap — including a bare "capability" implementation — is net-negative against the no-S null, because the null adds no ungraded writers.
- **Evidence:** `internal/agent/deps.go:61-96`; `internal/evidence/emitter.go:44-74`; `internal/agent/agent.go:246-253`; `internal/mutations/aggregate.go:28-68`; `internal/mutations/signal.go:17-46`; `internal/tool/quality_history.go:17-31`.
- **Suggestion:** Make it a disqualifying gate, not a criterion, in §Tournament criteria: *"A candidate is eliminated unless it names (a) the field that carries the grade on the emitted record, (b) the store or retrieval path that separates graded-speculative from validated, and (c) the filter applied at the aggregation boundary that promotes records into durable signal."* For the no-S null this is the specific case to argue: a capability with no authority ladder writes nothing new into the store, whereas a phase with a half-specified ladder writes ungraded records that Compound will aggregate — so the null is *safer*, and the evidence that would overturn that is a demonstrated grade field surviving hops (2)–(4) intact.

### expiry-names-no-actor-and-no-addressable-target

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** charter:39 ("expiration or review condition") as an emitted field of the scout report
- **What:** The promotion path is specified (probe → Reflect → Compound, charter:41) and the demotion path is a field on a document. No actor is named who performs demotion, no act is named that constitutes it, no store is named where the lowered grade is written, and no trigger is named that runs at expiry. The runtime makes this worse than a process gap: the only durable store is append-only (`appendJSONL` opens with `O_APPEND|O_CREATE|O_WRONLY` and syncs, `internal/evidence/emitter.go:52-74`), so a record cannot be edited in place; and `Evidence` has no identifier field (`internal/agent/deps.go:61-96`), so a compensating "re-grade" record would have nothing to point at. Citation scenario: a scout report with `expires: 2026-11-01` is written in August. In December an Orient turn retrieves it — from the session JSONL, from intercore, or from whatever index the implementer adds — and reads seven confident fields including a correspondence mapping and a prediction. Nothing in the artifact says it expired, because expiry was a timestamp and no act consumed it. Non-binding opinion has become precedent by the exact asymmetry the tradition warns about.
- **Evidence:** charter:39; `internal/evidence/emitter.go:52-74`; `internal/agent/deps.go:61-96`.
- **Suggestion:** Amend spec item 2 (charter:75-76) to require, for every candidate: *"the act that lowers the grade, the actor that performs it, the store where the lowering is recorded, and the identifier by which it names the artifact it lowers."* A second-order consequence worth stating in the charter: any candidate that emits a persistent artifact requires the emitted record to carry a stable id. This also names a candidate the seed list lacks and that this reasoning produces on its own: **Sunset** — an operation whose entire contract is re-grading, sweeping the store for claims past their review condition and writing an explicit demotion record for each. It emits no new claims, so it is the only candidate that cannot open a second truth channel.

### chain-severed-at-compound

- **Severity:** P1
- **Where:** charter:79 (item 4, "Re-entry path into OODARC"), charter:117 (required output item 5, "conditions under which it changes"), charter:41 ("only successful probes may later be Compounded")
- **What:** The charter requires forward routing and never requires backward traceability, so the chain terminates at the moment of promotion. In the runtime, promotion is `mutations.Aggregate` (`internal/agent/agent.go:246-253`), which reduces N records to sums and rates and writes a `QualitySignal` whose provenance fields are exactly three: session id, timestamp, phase (`internal/mutations/signal.go:17-25`; the hard signals are totals computed at `internal/mutations/aggregate.go:63-68`). No record ids, no probe reference, no source domain. Citation scenario: a scout report borrows a mechanism from control theory; a probe succeeds; the lesson compounds; four months later someone establishes that the source mechanism was misdescribed. There is no query — none can be written — that identifies which compounded signals depend on that borrowing, so nothing is re-graded and the discredited analogy keeps paying dividends. The asymmetry is that the charter's own §Required output asks for "conditions under which the recommendation changes" for the review, but asks nothing equivalent of the design it recommends.
- **Evidence:** `internal/agent/agent.go:246-253`; `internal/mutations/aggregate.go:28-68`; `internal/mutations/signal.go:17-46`.
- **Suggestion:** One added clause to item 4: *"and the provenance the compounded artifact retains — the list of record ids, probe ids, and source domains it derives from — such that discrediting any one of them yields the set of compounded claims to re-grade."* A candidate that cannot answer this scores zero on Epistemic safety regardless of how carefully its emitted report is specified.

### falsifiability-criterion-measures-probe-production-not-failure-attribution

- **Severity:** P2
- **Where:** charter:94 ("Falsifiability: produces probes whose failure can retire the hypothesis")
- **What:** The criterion measures a proxy. "Retire the hypothesis" is the local effect; the property that matters for transmitted knowledge is whether a failed probe is recorded *against the source domain and the analogy that motivated it*, so that a domain which repeatedly yields failed borrowings loses standing. The runtime shows exactly how the proxy fails: `ExperimentEvent` records `Type`, `Campaign`, `Hypothesis`, `Decision`, `Delta` (`internal/agent/deps.go:90-96`) — a complete local outcome record with no field naming where the hypothesis came from. A candidate can therefore score full marks on Falsifiability while its tenth failed transfer from the same source domain is indistinguishable from its first. Citation scenario: a reviewer a year later asks "has borrowing from ecology ever worked for us?" and the honest answer is that the data was never keyed that way.
- **Evidence:** charter:94; `internal/agent/deps.go:90-96`.
- **Suggestion:** Reword the criterion: *"Falsifiability: produces probes whose failure is recorded against both the hypothesis and the source-domain analogy that motivated it, such that a source domain's hit rate is computable."* Concretely that is one required field on the emitted report (`source_domain`) that must survive onto the probe outcome record.

### analogy-validity-satisfiable-by-surface-resemblance

- **Severity:** P2
- **Where:** charter:34-36 (the report's "correspondence mapping" and "where the analogy breaks" fields)
- **What:** These are the two fields that make Scout look rigorous, and neither carries a validity test. A correspondence mapping is satisfied by listing paired nouns; nothing requires naming the *shared mechanism* — the effective cause the transfer actually turns on — and nothing requires the named breakage to be the one that matters rather than a decorative caveat. Nor does the contract bar transfer from a source claim that is itself ungraded: a mechanism read off a blog post and a mechanism established in the system's own compounded record enter Scout on identical terms, and both leave carrying the same `authority: speculative` label — so the grade attaches to the *act* of scouting rather than to the *strength of the chain behind the claim*, which flattens a real distinction. Citation scenario: two scout reports are retrieved side by side; one rests on a validated internal mechanism, one on a magazine analogy; nothing distinguishes them and the reader treats them as peers. (This is also the substance of the Scout-vs-Speculate probe at charter:102: the dispute is not about outward behavior versus epistemic status, it is about whether the grade travels with the artifact or with the act — Scout as drawn grades the act.)
- **Evidence:** charter:34-36, charter:38, charter:102.
- **Suggestion:** Two clauses on the report contract: *"correspondence mapping must name the shared effective cause, not a list of resembling parts"* and *"the report must carry the grade of its source claim, and the emitted grade may not exceed it."* This also yields a second grading-derived candidate the seed list lacks: **Corroborate** — an operation whose entire contract is raising a grade only on independent support from a second, structurally distinct chain, which directly answers the single-source question the charter never asks (may one successful probe license compounding?).

## Verdict

The charter's central safety claim — that scout reports cannot become evidence — is asserted at charter:41 and enforced by nothing, and the runtime it would land in has one ungraded store, an append-only writer, records with no identifiers, and a Compound step that aggregates every line it can parse into the signal Orient later reads. That is a P0 that survives every candidate: any design that adds a writer without adding a grade field is worse than the no-S null. Below it sit two one-directional mechanisms — promotion specified without demotion, forward routing specified without a chain back — both repairable by three added clauses in the per-candidate spec rather than by re-running the tournament.
